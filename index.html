Community tutoring · Northwest Georgia

Academic support for Walker County students

We are a local tutoring group based in Walker County, Georgia, helping middle and high school students build confidence in math, science, English, and test prep. Sessions are led by experienced tutors and peer mentors who understand Georgia Standards of Excellence and the needs of our community.

Homework Help

After-school support in Algebra, Geometry, Biology, Chemistry, and ELA. Drop-in and scheduled sessions for Walker County Schools students.

EOC & Milestone Prep

Focused review for Georgia End-of-Course and Milestone assessments, with practice materials aligned to state standards.

SAT / ACT Coaching

Small-group and one-on-one test prep for college-bound students from LaFayette, Ridgeland, Heritage, and surrounding areas.

Parent Resources

Guides for HOPE Scholarship, dual enrollment at Georgia Northwestern, and connecting with school counselors in Walker County.

Schools we support

LaFayette High School · Ridgeland High School · Heritage High School · Chattooga (neighboring) · Walker County middle schools · Chickamauga City Schools · Rossville & Rock Spring communities

About our group

Walker County Tutoring Group is a volunteer and peer-led initiative dedicated to closing learning gaps and celebrating academic growth. We are not affiliated with Walker County Schools or the Georgia Department of Education; we partner with families and community spaces to offer accessible help close to home.
